The short version

Eclipsebound is a single-player game. It creates an anonymous player account so your progress can be backed up, stores your game save in the cloud, and nothing else. We don't run ads, we don't sell data, and we don't use third-party analytics or tracking.

What we collect and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
An anonymous player ID (a random identifier created by Firebase Authentication when you first play)To store your save under an account, and so support can find your save if you send us the IDGoogle Firebase (Authentication), US servers
Your game save (heroes, progress, settings, play time, the device name it was last played on, and a few one-time progress milestones such as "first summon")To back up your progress, restore it on a new device, and resolve conflicts between two devicesGoogle Firebase (Cloud Firestore), US servers
Account link (only if you choose to link Apple or Google sign-in)To let you recover the same account on another deviceGoogle Firebase; Apple or Google handle the sign-in itself under their own policies
Purchase receipts (pack, time, store transaction id; never card details)To grant and restore what you bought, and to verify purchasesApple / Google issue them; a copy is stored with your save in Google Firebase
Crash and basic device information that Apple's App Store or Google Play may share with usTo fix bugsApple / Google, under their policies; we see it only in aggregate

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, advertising identifier, or anything from other apps. If you email support, we keep that email thread to answer you.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad identifiers.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data with anyone except the service providers above, who process it only to run the game.

In-app purchases

Eclipsebound sells Eclipse Crystals and bundles (DESIGN.md §31). Purchases are made through Apple's App Store or Google Play, who handle payment under their own policies; we never see your payment details. We receive a purchase receipt (which pack, when, a transaction identifier) to grant the items, to restore your purchases on a new device, and to verify purchases against our records. Receipts are stored with your save. Summons that cost Crystals show their odds in the game.

How long we keep it

Your account and save stay while you play. Delete my account (Settings → Account) deletes the account and the save after a 7-day grace period, during which you can cancel. Old conflict backups are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without an account: the game needs the anonymous account only for cloud backup; if you don't want it, don't sign in to the stores' services and [describe the opt-out if one exists, or remove this line].
  • Delete everything: Settings → Account → Delete my account, or email us your player ID.
  • See your data: email us your player ID and we'll send a copy of your save.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, and to complain to a regulator. Email us and we'll help.

Children

Eclipsebound is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. The game contains no chat, no user-generated content and no ads. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Where data is stored and transfers

Our servers (Google Firebase) are in the United States. If you play from elsewhere, your data is transferred there, protected by the service provider's standard contractual safeguards.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS). Server rules let each player read and write only their own save. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting you we'll tell you as the law requires.

Changes

We'll post changes here and update the effective date. For material changes, the game will show a notice.

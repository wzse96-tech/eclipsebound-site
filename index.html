Every hero answers one moon

Each moon has its own color, temper and power. Build a team whose moons cover each other, and the battle tilts your way.

Tide MoonWater · patience, healing
Ember MoonFire · courage, the forge
Verdant MoonNature · growth, endurance
Dawn MoonLight · hope, protection
Dusk MoonDark · dreams, secrets

Water beats Fire, Fire beats Nature, Nature beats Water. Light and Dark each overpower the other.

What you'll do

Turn-based battles you can play by hand or on Auto, a world map of ten regions, and a home that grows with you.

Collect and awaken

Summon heroes, level and ascend them, then awaken them into a new look, a new name and a stronger signature skill.

Battles with timing

Speed fills each hero's turn bar. Save a big skill for the boss, strip a shield before your burst, and keep your healer alive.

The Eclipse

Summon the eclipse from the Observatory and descend into an endless dungeon where nothing heals between waves.

Moru, your home

Build your camp on the back of a gentle sky whale. Your heroes walk around it, and it lights up as you rebuild.

Moonglyphs

Socket glyphs in sets, chase the right main stat, and tune a team that's truly yours.

Friends and guilds

Send daily gifts, borrow a friend's best hero for tough stages, and take on guild bosses together.

Meet Kestra

A young archer with a quick draw and a quicker grin. She answers all five moons, and each awakening gives her a new name.
